# Concept note v0.1

Status: proposal. Date: 2 October 2026. [Scope](SCOPE.md) · [Index](README.md)

## Definition and purpose

TUN Systemic Evolution is a proposed discipline and protocol for evidence-led, human-governed development of people, AI systems, products, and organizations. It connects experience to assessed learning, assessed learning to an authorized change, and that change to subsequent observation.

Its central question is: **What should change in what we know, how we work, and what we build—and what evidence would justify that change?**

Evolution completes the TUN family by making learning reusable and accountable across Design, Engineering, and Management. It runs during discovery, delivery, operation, and retirement. There is no requirement to finish the other three before learning starts.

## Five distinct meanings of learning

| Process | What changes | Evidence needed |
|---|---|---|
| Human development | Skill, judgment, understanding, practice | Performance on appropriate new tasks; delayed assessment where retention matters; participant feedback |
| Context and memory | Information made available to a later interaction | Correct scope, provenance, freshness, inspectability, and permitted reuse |
| AI system adaptation | Prompt, retrieval, model selection, tools, workflow, interface | Versioned comparison and regression checks within the intended setting |
| Model training | Learned model parameters | Training-data permissions, reproducible evaluation, model lineage, and a separate release process |
| Organizational learning | Shared assumptions, policy, routines, roles | Evidence of changed practice and relevant outcomes, with responsible decisions |

The pilots implement none of the learning mechanisms themselves. They exercise a narrow decision process over declared synthetic measurements and configuration versions. The family pilot also links a Management decision to a verified Engineering publication and a separate local activation. This tests record handling before claiming capability improvement.

## Distinctive contributions

**A learning contract.** Every material lesson identifies the assumption, evidence, limitations, applicable context, and decision it can inform. A fluent retrospective remains a proposed interpretation until assessed.

**A human development track.** People practice, compare reasoning, receive feedback, and assess transfer. Evaluation includes the ability to challenge and direct the AI. Assisted performance and unaided competence remain separate observations.

**An adaptation record.** A candidate identifies its exact changes and dependencies, the evidence reviewed, who adopted it, the authority used to activate it, and a restoration path.

**Selective reuse.** Lessons carry applicability and expiry conditions. A result in one language, team, model version, or customer population does not automatically transfer to another.

**An evidence bridge.** Engineering effects, product quality, human development, and organizational outcomes can be linked without being treated as equivalent claims.

## Decision boundaries

Management already owns reviews, `LearningRecord`, policy, and adaptation decisions. Evolution elaborates the experimental method and evidence supporting those responsibilities. Use the existing management learning record or a versioned reference; do not create two competing organizational decisions.

Engineering already requires regression evaluation and prohibits learned preferences from granting authority. Evolution provides evaluation plans and candidate evidence; the engineering host still authorizes every effectful deployment or recovery action.

Design already exposes sources, uncertainty, memory, proposals, decisions, and recovery. Evolution gives those views a learning context and adds patterns only when a real adopter needs them.

## A proportionate practice

Use short records for small, reversible changes. Combine signal, reflection, and hypothesis in one record when that preserves meaning. Use more rigorous comparison, independent review, and staged exposure where consequences or uncertainty warrant them. Research-grade experiments are not required for every correction, and a weak comparison cannot justify a strong causal claim.

The useful outputs are improved capability, corrected assumptions, rejected ineffective changes, and retired obsolete practices. More records, more experiments, or more autonomous behavior are not success measures by themselves.

## What a reusable lesson contains

Use a short, inspectable statement: **In this context, we observed this result using this method; it supports this conclusion within these limits; it changes this decision or practice; we will reconsider it under these conditions.** Link the supporting and contrary evidence rather than copying restricted source material into every record.

A lesson may be proposed, assessed, superseded, or retired. Reuse requires an applicability check. Keeping a lesson available does not make it perpetually correct, and retrieving it does not authorize following it as an instruction.

## First slice and expansion

Start with a support-drafting workflow that leaves publication with the human. Compare two pinned prompt configurations using fixed evaluation definitions, bounded evidence, an explicit decision, local activation, and a regression-triggered restoration. Include a human error-detection measure so faster output alone cannot pass.

The first connected slice now uses actual pinned Management and Engineering code, with Design components for a read-only report. Its verified effect is publication of a local adoption manifest, not deployment of a model. The [family guide](FAMILY-PILOT.md) names the exact boundaries and remaining failure cases.

Next, run one real learning cycle with participant involvement and, where human learning is claimed, an appropriate transfer or delayed assessment. Expand toward reusable practice libraries, training pipelines, cross-team lesson exchange, or automated experiment proposal only when the first adoption reveals a need.

An AI may suggest changes and analyze permitted evidence. Permission to investigate, permission to experiment, permission to adopt, and permission to activate are separate. No improvement score grants any of them automatically.
