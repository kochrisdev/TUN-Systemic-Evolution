# Manifesto v0.1

**Learn deliberately. Change responsibly. Evolve together.**

These commitments describe the intended practice. They are not claims that the current pilots demonstrate human learning or production readiness. Apply them through the [first learning cycle](FIRST-LEARNING-CYCLE.md) and assess them against the [draft requirements](SPECIFICATION-v0.1.md).

## 01 — Begin with human purpose

Define the benefit with the people affected. Quality, agency, sustainable workload, and resilience matter alongside speed and cost. People retain the right to challenge the definition of improvement.

## 02 — Follow the whole system

A local optimization can move work or risk elsewhere. Inspect the relationships among people, models, tools, incentives, policies, and outcomes.

## 03 — Make learning a supported claim

Name the assumption, evidence, contrary observations, context, and uncertainty. Distinguish an observation from an interpretation and an interpretation from an established result.

## 04 — Practice and test

Create opportunities for human practice and bounded system experiments. Declare what would disconfirm the hypothesis. Negative results can prevent costly repetition.

## 05 — Develop human and AI capability together

Evaluate human judgment, skill retention, and appropriate reliance as well as AI task performance. Let people shape their development and contest assessments.

## 06 — Protect context and choice

Make the purposes of feedback, evaluation, memory, and training explicit. Respect participation choices and data boundaries. Preserve the conditions under which a lesson is useful.

## 07 — Preserve disagreement

Keep conflicting evidence and affected people's perspectives visible. A second model's agreement is not independent human accountability.

## 08 — Adopt from proportionate evidence

Match the strength of a claim and the scope of adoption to the quality of its evidence. A small trial can justify a further trial without justifying unrestricted release.

## 09 — Keep learning separate from authority

Feedback, memory, confidence, and success cannot expand permissions. Material changes to autonomy, policy, data use, or mandate return through the responsible decision process.

## 10 — Preserve history and recovery

Version the change, dependencies, decision, and activation. Prepare restoration and disclose what cannot be undone. Returning a configuration to an earlier version does not erase its prior effects.

## 11 — Revisit what was learned

Monitor after adoption. Environments, populations, models, and incentives change. Give lessons review triggers, and retire those that no longer apply.

## 12 — Learn how to learn

Assess whether the learning process improves decisions at an acceptable cost. Change the method when it creates bureaucracy, hides uncertainty, or erodes human capability.
