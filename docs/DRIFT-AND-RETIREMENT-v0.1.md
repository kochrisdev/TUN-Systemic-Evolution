# Drift and retirement v0.1

Status: proposed guidance.

Give every adopted change a monitoring owner, observation scope, collection interval or event trigger, freshness limit, response expectation, and next review. Watch the outcome, human burden, relevant subgroups, interventions, failure modes, and total cost selected for the decision.

Trigger review when a model, tool, data source, language, population, workload, policy, or key assumption changes. Evidence can become inapplicable without any metric crossing a threshold. Missing evidence is a separate condition with a declared response.

Use current Management authority to request containment and Engineering evidence to determine what stopped. Group repeated notices about an unchanged condition. Notify on a material change, breached condition, required decision, or observed recovery rather than emitting a new retrospective for every event.

Retire lessons as well as software. Mark applicability withdrawn or superseded; link the reason and replacement when there is one. Review dependent lessons and configurations. Disable future work under retired mandates, account for unresolved effects, revoke relevant credentials or delegations, and retain only the records required and permitted.

For the standalone pilot, monitoring is a manually supplied synthetic observation. A breach creates a review result; it never silently grants permission to roll back. The fixture owner then requests a supported restoration. Scheduling, notifications, automatic rollback, and provider containment are future integrations.

The family report is a point-in-time snapshot, not continuing monitoring. Its publication receipt describes an operation's historical effect; first activation also checks the current publication resource. After activation, the bridge does not schedule rechecks or automatically coordinate withdrawal and rollback.

A retired lesson should remain discoverable as retired where its history is still permitted to be retained. State why it no longer applies so that a later search does not revive it as current guidance.
