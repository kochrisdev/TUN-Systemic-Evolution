# Governance

TUN Systemic Evolution is maintained by Christopher Tun (`@kochrisdev`), maintainer of the companion TUN repositories. The framework and reference pilots were developed with AI assistance. The specification remains a draft, not a stable standard.

## Decisions and accountability

Governance is maintainer-led and open to contributions. The maintainer accepts framework changes; adopting organizations remain accountable for their experiments, participants, data, and operational decisions. Acceptance into this repository does not grant an adopter's agents runtime authority.

Record consequential framework decisions with rationale, alternatives, affected requirements, evidence, and migration implications. Identify conflicts of interest and limits of independent review. A small team may combine roles while recording the overlap.

Management owns organizational priorities and adoption decisions. Evolution owns the learning method and evidence within the chosen scope; Engineering enforces effectful authority; Design makes evidence and controls understandable. These responsibilities can be held in one team without collapsing their distinct claims.

## Changes and evidence

Treat material protocol or profile changes as reviewable revisions. Keep the twelve draft requirement identifiers stable when refining wording; explain any changed meaning. Preserve historical validation results and distinguish them from newer checks.

Before expanding scope, seek feedback from people doing and affected by the work. Evaluate review burden and allow challenges to the definition of improvement. No independent certification program or validated organizational adoption is claimed.
