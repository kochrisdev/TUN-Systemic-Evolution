# Governance

TUN Systemic Evolution is maintained by Christopher Tun (`@kochrisdev`), the maintainer of the companion TUN repositories. The initial documentation and reference pilot were developed with AI assistance. The specification remains a draft, not a stable standard.

Governance is maintainer-led and open to contributions. The maintainer accepts changes to this framework; adopting organizations retain accountability for their own experiments, human participation, data, and operational decisions. Framework acceptance never grants an adopter's agents runtime authority.

Keep consequential framework decisions with their rationale, alternatives, affected requirements, evidence, and migration implications. Clearly identify conflicts of interest and the limits of independent review. A small team may combine roles while recording that overlap.

No independent certification program or validated organizational adoption is claimed.
